    What remains

    In the old stories, Shesha is the serpent who stays when everything else dissolves at the end of a cycle. He is what remains, and the worlds rest on him until they begin again. I like having a name that means that.

    You found all three seals, so here are the things that didn’t fit anywhere else.

    On losing

    I have a very specific talent for finishing fourth: butterfly, freestyle, relay, twice each at Khelo India Inter-State. Close enough to the podium to hear the anthem. You learn more from fourth than from first. You learn that the work goes on.
